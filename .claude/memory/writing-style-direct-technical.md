---
name: writing-style-direct-technical
description: Write direct technical prose; no colloquialisms, slang, metaphor, or chatty language
metadata:
  type: feedback
---

All user-facing writing must be short, direct technical prose. Banned: colloquialisms, slang, metaphor, and chatty phrasing of any kind. Specific phrases called out as unacceptable: "worth a glance at", "what landed", "punchline", "calling out", and anything similar. Use plain equivalents such as "check" or "key point". Never use the word "honest" (including constructions like "honest framing"). Chatty offers are also banned: write "do you want me to ..." rather than "say the word". Reduce verbosity generally.

**Why:** The user reads output as technical documentation, not conversation; informal phrasing adds length and obscures the content.

**How to apply:** Applies to chat responses, commit messages, docs, and code comments. Before sending, scan for idioms and filler and replace them with the literal statement. See also [[no-em-dashes]].
